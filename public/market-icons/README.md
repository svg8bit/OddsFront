# Market strip icons

These are local, transparent vector assets used as monochrome UI glyphs. They
are served from the OddsFront CDN; the browser does not fetch third-party icon
hosts.

- Bitcoin: transparent Bootstrap Icons `currency-bitcoin` vector; it is the
  standalone mark without a circle. Bootstrap Icons is MIT licensed.
- Ethereum and NVIDIA: transparent vector brand marks from Simple Icons; the
  upstream project distributes its icon data under CC0 1.0.
- Apple and the standalone SpaceX X: clean vector marks based on Wikimedia
  Commons versions of the company logos. Trademark rights remain with their
  respective owners.
- Gold, Silver, and Copper deliberately share the same transparent bullion mark
  requested for the compact strip. Brent crude oil and S&P 500 use their
  DropsTab asset SVGs with only the full-canvas color tile removed.

Color is applied consistently by the market strip CSS. None of the icons has a
card, circle tile, or opaque background layer.
